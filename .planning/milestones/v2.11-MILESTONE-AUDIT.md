### Finding — WARNING, found and CLOSED during this audit

**`isEventoCritico()` used `Evento.dataFim` while `listEventos` (GET /eventos, Agenda, AGD-34/35) and `AlertasDiariosJob` both used `Evento.dataInicio`** — a genuine 5th divergent "prazo crítico" implementation that AGD-34/AGD-35's own stated goal ("deixa de existir qualquer 5ª implementação divergente de prazo crítico") was supposed to eliminate. For any multi-day event where `dataInicio`/`dataFim` land in different risk buckets, the Dashboard's `prazos_vencer`/`prazos_criticos_count` KPIs would disagree with the Agenda page and the daily alert job for the identical row.

This was explicitly flagged by Phase 92's own code review (`92-REVIEW.md` CR-01) and named in `92-CONTEXT.md`'s Deferred Ideas as a candidate for "Phase 97 (auditoria de milestone)" — but Phase 97's own AUD-05 fresh-audit task scoped itself narrowly to the notification-track files (`NotificacaoService.java`/`NotificacaoController.java`/`AlertasDiariosJob.java`) and never touched `ResourceController`'s Evento-risco area, so the deferred item fell out of every closeout document (STATE.md, PROJECT.md, REQUIREMENTS.md) until this audit's integration-checker independently rediscovered it via fresh source comparison.

**Fixed directly during this audit** (not deferred further): `isEventoCritico()` standardized on `dataInicio`, matching the other two call sites. Full backend suite re-confirmed green (69/69) after the change. Commit `f0c62ff`; tracking updated in `STATE.md` (commit `305de65`).
