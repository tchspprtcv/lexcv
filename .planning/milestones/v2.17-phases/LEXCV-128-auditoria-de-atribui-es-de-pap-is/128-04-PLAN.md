Rewrite the guard's doc-comment to record the race closure (Portuguese):

- this is the same lock technique as ParecerSolicitacaoRepository (WR-04, Phase 87).

- only paths that go through this guard are serialized. They are the only paths that can reduce active holders: createUser only adds, and deleteRole refuses the protected role;
