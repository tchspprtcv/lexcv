<objective>
Turn `GET/PUT /api/v1/admin/rbac` into an office-scoped surface over `t_tenant_role`, and retire
Phase 121's ISOL-03 gate **in writing** (127-CONTEXT.md Decisão 1).

Phase 121 closed `PUT /admin/rbac` to `PLATAFORMA_ADMIN` for a reason that was correct at the time:
`Role` and `Permission` were global tables with no `tenant_id`, so an office administrator saving
that matrix rewrote the very rows every other office depended on. Phases 125 and 126 removed that
condition — each office now owns its roles in `t_tenant_role`, and authority resolution already reads
from there. This plan is not undoing Phase 121; it is satisfying the condition that made Phase 121
temporary. The `ISOL-03` comment above `updateRbac` explains exactly why the gate exists, so its
replacement must explain why it no longer needs to, or the next reader will conclude a security
guard was deleted by accident.

Gate and body change in the same task, never in sequence: opening the gate while the body still
writes global rows would reopen the cross-tenant write, and tenant-scoping the body while the gate
still says `PLATAFORMA_ADMIN` would leave a platform operator writing office rows.

Purpose: PAPEL-01, PAPEL-03, PAPEL-07 and PAPEL-08 become real behaviour, with isolation proven by
test rather than asserted by comment.
Output: an office-scoped read model, an id-keyed tenant-scoped write with the floor-lock rule, and a
test file that stands up two tenants and proves the second one never moves.
</objective>
