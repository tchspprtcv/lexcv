<objective>
Make `rbac:manage` and `users:manage` the authorities that actually govern `/api/v1/admin`, and
land the office-scoped DTO contract that plans 03, 04 and 05 write against.

This plan carries a finding that neither 127-CONTEXT.md nor 127-PATTERNS.md surfaced, and it is a
hard prerequisite for PAPEL-04/PAPEL-08, not a nicety: **`AdminController`'s class-level gate is
`@PreAuthorize("hasRole('ADMIN')")` (line 32).** `UserPrincipal.create` derives authorities as
`"ROLE_" + roleName` from the *effective* role names, which since Phase 126 are the office's
`TenantRole` names. The moment an office renames its administrator role — the very capability
PAPEL-04 ships in this phase — that user's authorities become `ROLE_<new name>`, `hasRole('ADMIN')`
stops matching, and the office administrator is locked out of the entire `/admin` surface: user
management AND the RBAC screen they just used to rename the role. That is precisely the self-lockout
threat this phase is required to prevent (PAPEL-08), and it is silent: a 403 with no explanation.

The fix is the same fix CATL-04 asks for, applied one level wider: gate by permission, not by role
name. Permissions survive renaming because they are snapshotted onto the `TenantRole` itself, and
plan 03's floor-lock rule guarantees the office's administrator role can never lose `rbac:manage`
or `users:manage`.

Deliberate sequencing note: this plan does NOT touch `updateRbac`'s gate. `updateRbac` still writes
the **global** `Role`/`Permission` tables until plan 03 rewrites its body to be tenant-scoped.
Opening that gate to `rbac:manage` before the body is tenant-scoped would re-open, for the duration
of one wave, exactly the ISOL-03 cross-tenant write that Phase 121 closed. Gate and body change
together, in plan 03.

Purpose: authority-based gates + the shared read/write contract, before any behaviour changes.
Output: four DTOs, one count query with an integration test, two gate changes, and a rewritten
authorization test that proves the gates by real proxy rather than by reading annotations.
</objective>
