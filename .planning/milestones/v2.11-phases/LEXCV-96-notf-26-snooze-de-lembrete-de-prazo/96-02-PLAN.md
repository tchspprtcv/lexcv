<objective>
Prove NOTF-26's read-side visibility contract against a real PostgreSQL Testcontainer: a
future-snoozed notification is invisible to the badge-count and mark-all-read queries, and reappears
automatically once its snoozedUntil timestamp has passed — with no job, no new row, no dedup
interaction. This is the "looks done but isn't" guard for a query-level visibility filter (a class of
bug the milestone's own PITFALLS checklist calls out), and it directly evidences success criteria 2
(disappears while snoozed) and 3 (reappears after expiry) at the data layer.

This plan extends the existing `NotificacaoRepositoryIT` (created in Phase 91 / TEST-01) rather than
introducing new infrastructure. It has zero source-file overlap with the frontend plan (96-03) and
runs in the same wave, in parallel.

Purpose: NOTF-26 success criteria 2 and 3 (data-layer evidence).
Output: added test methods in `NotificacaoRepositoryIT`.
</objective>
