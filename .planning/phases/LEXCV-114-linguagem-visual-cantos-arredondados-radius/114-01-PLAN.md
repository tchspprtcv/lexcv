<objective>
Reverse the deliberate "sharp corner / institutional document" look established in v2.13 by making the whole app render rounded corners consistently (RAD-01, ROADMAP Success Criteria 1-3). This is TWO inseparable moves:

1. Flip the shared `--radius` design token from `0rem` to `0.5rem` (8px, shadcn's "Default" preset) in both `web/src/app/globals.css` and `webpage/src/app/globals.css`.
2. Remove the hardcoded `rounded-none` overrides that today make that token change invisible almost everywhere. Codebase research (114-UI-SPEC.md "Critical Scope Finding") proved that `cn()` uses `tailwind-merge`, so a literal `rounded-none` on a className deterministically beats the primitive's own token-derived default. Editing only the two globals.css files would leave Success Criteria 2 and 3 false on most screens. Confirmed current counts (re-run at plan time): `web/src` = 270 `rounded-none` lines across 28 files, `webpage/src` = 7 across 5 files.

Purpose: Close RAD-01 for real, not just at the token level. The token flip without the sweep is cosmetically inert; the sweep without the token flip reveals sharp corners. They ship together.

Output: 32 modified files (2 globals.css + 25 web/src call sites + 5 webpage/src files). The 3 web/ exception files (calendar/input-group/tabs) are deliberately NOT modified. Mechanical proof of done: `grep -rn "rounded-none" web/src webpage/src` returns exactly 6 lines.
</objective>
