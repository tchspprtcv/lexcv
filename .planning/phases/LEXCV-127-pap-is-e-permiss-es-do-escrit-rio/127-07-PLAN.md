<objective>
Rewrite `RbacTab` from a read-only matrix of four hardcoded global roles into the office's own
editable role console, exactly as the approved UI-SPEC specifies.

This is the customer-facing half of the phase, and the UI-SPEC is binding — it passed all six
checker dimensions and it resolves, with reasons, every design question this screen raises (why no
"what changed" banner, why two neutral badges instead of one, why rename is a Dialog, why the delete
refusal is static in-menu text rather than a tooltip on a disabled item, why there is no amber in
this phase, and why the save flow has no confirmation dialog unlike `/plataforma/moldes`). Do not
re-litigate those; implement them.

Two rules in it are security, not styling. The matrix locks by the server-computed `protegido` flag,
never by the literal string `"ADMIN"` — the current code's `if (role === "ADMIN") return;` guard
stops working the first time an office renames that role, which this phase makes possible. And the
floor-lock rule (for the protected role only, a permission it currently holds is locked checked; one
it does not hold stays freely grantable) is what keeps the office from removing its own access to
this very screen — the client mirror of plan 03's server-side refusal.

Purpose: PAPEL-01 through PAPEL-05 and PAPEL-08 become something an administrator can actually do.
Output: two new presentational components and a rewritten tab.
</objective>
