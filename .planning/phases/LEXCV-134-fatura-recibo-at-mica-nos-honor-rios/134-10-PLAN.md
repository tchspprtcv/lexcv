<objective>
Protect fiscal documents from deletion paths and keep them linked through client merges: 409 guards on delete pagamento faturado / cliente / processo / honorário with documents, row locks that serialize these operations with in-flight emissions (extended lock order), and merge re-pointing of `cliente_id`. Prove the races on real PostgreSQL.

Purpose: EMIS-09. Without row locks the guards are check-then-act and an emission committing between check and delete leaves an orphan document (RESEARCH Pattern 2, research_resolutions 1).
Output: ResourceController handler changes, Mockito guard test, Testcontainers race IT.
</objective>
