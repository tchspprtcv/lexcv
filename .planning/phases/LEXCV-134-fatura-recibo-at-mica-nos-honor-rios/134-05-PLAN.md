<objective>
Create the read side of Phase 134 and the guard/merge helpers: a single `DocumentoFiscalService` that lists and details documents per tenant, answers "has documents?" for the delete guards, re-points documents on client merge, and maps payments to their document references; plus the response DTOs the controllers and frontend consume.

Purpose: EMIS-11 (listing/detail), EMIS-12 (payments without document shown as such) and EMIS-09 (guards/merge) all need these queries. Routing them through one service keeps `ResourceController` free of fiscal repositories, so the CFG-03 source guard (plan 08) stays simple.
Output: 4 DTOs, DocumentoFiscalService, DocumentoFiscalServiceTest.
</objective>
